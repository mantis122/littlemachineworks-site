Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Little Machine Works respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SVG Vector Converter handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

SVG Vector Converter does not require a user account and does not intentionally collect personal information from users.

Files you open or convert

SVG files and other files you choose in the app are processed on your device for conversion, preview, validation, reporting, and export. Little Machine Works does not intentionally upload the contents of those files to its own servers.

Advertising

SVG Vector Converter does not contain advertising.

Purchases

If paid features are offered through Google Play, purchase processing is handled by Google Play. Little Machine Works does not receive or store your full payment card information. Google may process information associated with a purchase under Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Analytics and tracking

Little Machine Works does not intentionally use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral advertising technologies in SVG Vector Converter.

Data sharing

Little Machine Works does not sell personal information collected through SVG Vector Converter. Because the app is designed to perform its core conversion work locally on the device, Little Machine Works does not intentionally share the contents of user-selected SVG files with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Little Machine Works does not maintain user accounts or an application database containing the contents of files you convert. Files you save or export remain under your control on your device or in the storage location you choose. You can delete those files using your device or storage provider.

Children's privacy

SVG Vector Converter is a general-purpose utility and is not directed specifically to children. Little Machine Works does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the app to protect information handled by the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as SVG Vector Converter changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
